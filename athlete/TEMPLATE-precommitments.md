# Precommitments

> **The athlete writes this file. Not the coach.**
>
> These are the arguments their future, more impatient, more discouraged self will need to
> hear — written now, while thinking clearly. A precommitment in the coach's voice has no
> force; the sentences must be theirs.
>
> The coach quotes the relevant line back **verbatim** when the situation fires
> (CLAUDE.md §2), and does not paraphrase it.

## Format

> When ___ happens → remind me that ___.

## Categories to prompt with

Prompt with these, then write down what they say. Not every category will apply.

- **After a bad measurement** — a weigh-in, a lab result, a flare, a failed session
- **When they want to go faster** than the plan's stated safe rate
- **After a planned indulgence** — the "I'll make up for it tomorrow" impulse
- **When motivation drops** and the plan starts feeling pointless
- **When they want to add something** to a plan that isn't being followed yet
- **When a value is under pressure** and deleting it looks like the easy fix
- **When they want to quit** — what they'd want to be reminded of
