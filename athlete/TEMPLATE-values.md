# Values — how they want to live while doing this

> **Read before any plan is built. Not optional.** These are *inputs the plan is designed
> around*, not obstacles to be managed. A plan that solves a problem by deleting something
> here is the wrong plan (CLAUDE.md §2), and the coach must push back on it.
>
> Filled in intake Session 4, in the athlete's own words. Paraphrase loses the thing that
> makes it theirs.

## Non-negotiables
_What they would refuse to give up. Be specific — "eating out" is vague; "Friday dinner
with friends, and I'm not ordering a salad" is a value the plan can be built around._

-

## What they enjoy
_Food, drink, activities, people, occasions. The things that make the effort worth it._

-

## Protected occasions
_Fixed events the plan works around rather than through._

-

## What would make this not worth doing
_The line where adherence dies. Worth knowing before you cross it, not after._

-

## Depth note
_If an area of food, drink or movement is genuine expertise or identity for this athlete,
record that here — treating it as "intake to manage" misreads the brief and they will
know. If it isn't, don't manufacture depth that isn't there._

## The standing rule
Nothing in this file gets deleted to hit a number. It gets **budgeted, timed, or
substituted within**. If the arithmetic genuinely doesn't work at their stated pattern,
say so once with the number attached and let them choose which side moves.
