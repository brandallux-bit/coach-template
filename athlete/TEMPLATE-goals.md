## Guardrails — apply at every ranking

- **Safety is not in the ranking.** It is the floor under all of it (CLAUDE.md §5),
  including everything in `athlete/hard-constraints.md`.
- **Adherence is not in the ranking either.** It multiplies whatever is ranked first. A
  worse plan followed beats a better plan abandoned.
- _Domain-specific floors — e.g. a strength floor protecting lifts during a deficit_
- _Injury constraints, if `injury-history.md` exists_
- **Lifestyle:** nothing in `values.md` gets deleted to hit a number.
